# Privacy Policy for BlackoutHorrorVR

Last updated: October 2026

RescoVR ("we", "our", or "us") operates the BlackoutHorrorVR application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our game.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
BlackoutHorrorVR is built for the Meta Horizon platform. 
* **Personal Data:** We do not independently collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
* **Meta Platform Data:** The game may utilize basic platform data provided by Meta Horizon OS (such as your Oculus/Meta User ID or Username) solely to enable gameplay functionality, local save files, achievements, or leaderboard features. This data remains on your device or within Meta's secure ecosystem.

## 2. Log Data and Analytics
We do not track your gameplay habits or collect diagnostic data on external servers. Any crash reports or performance metrics are handled securely through Meta’s built-in developer reporting tools.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our game runs on the Meta Horizon platform. Meta may collect data in accordance with their own Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Meta's Privacy Policy to understand how they manage your hardware and account data.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our games are not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
